## 2. The 5-Step WGU Exam Problem-Solving Algorithm

Whenever you face a Calculus problem on an exam, execute this deterministic 5-step algorithm:

1. **Step 1 (Identify the Goal):** Are you finding a **Rate of Change** (Derivative $\frac{dy}{dx}$), an **Accumulated Area/Work** (Integral $\int$), or a **Maximum/Minimum Optimization** (Set $\frac{dy}{dx} = 0$)?
2. **Step 2 (Deconstruct the Function):** Is it a single polynomial (Power Rule), a product of two terms (Product Rule), a fraction (Quotient Rule), or a nested function (Chain Rule: *Derivative of the outside $\times$ Derivative of the inside*)?
3. **Step 3 (Execute Algebraic Differentiation/Integration):** Apply the precise formula cleanly without skipping steps.
4. **Step 4 (Evaluate Critical Points or Limits):** If optimizing, solve for $x$ where $f'(x) = 0$. If evaluating definite integrals, compute $F(b) - F(a)$.
5. **Step 5 (Ground Truth Physical Check):** Does the unit and magnitude make physical sense? (e.g., velocity is $m/s$, energy is Joules, resistance is Ohms).
